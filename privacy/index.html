Privacy Policy for Twaddle

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Twaddle is a language-learning application operated by Reality Studios LLC, a United States company (“Reality Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and process when you use Twaddle, including the mobile application listed as Twaddle: Language Learning, how we use that information, when it may be shared with service providers, and the choices and rights available to you.

By using Twaddle, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide and operate Twaddle.

Account and Authentication Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Your email address;
  • Authentication information associated with your account;
  • An internal user or account identifier; and
  • Information necessary to authenticate you through supported sign-in methods.

Twaddle currently supports email-based authentication, Google Sign-In, and Sign in with Apple. If you use Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, the authentication provider may provide us with information necessary to authenticate and associate your account, subject to your settings with that provider.

We do not require your phone number or physical address to create a Twaddle account.

Profile and Language-Learning Information

We collect and store information necessary to personalize your learning experience and track your progress. This may include:

  • Your native language;
  • The language you are learning;
  • Your proficiency level;
  • Your learning goals;
  • App preferences;
  • Onboarding status;
  • Microphone or voice-recording consent status and related timestamps;
  • Lesson progress;
  • Words and expressions you have encountered;
  • Vocabulary mastery and spaced-repetition information;
  • Review history and scheduling information;
  • Lesson performance, such as known and unknown word counts, lesson ratings, translation reliance, and completion information;
  • Context associated with vocabulary learning, such as the most recent lesson sentence or meaning in which a word was encountered; and
  • Information about lessons or sections downloaded for offline use.

This information is used to provide and personalize Twaddle’s language-learning features.

Microphone and Pronunciation Audio

Twaddle includes optional pronunciation features that require access to your device’s microphone.

When you choose to use a pronunciation feature, Twaddle temporarily records your speech and transmits the audio to Microsoft Azure Speech Services for pronunciation assessment. Microsoft Azure analyzes the audio and returns pronunciation feedback to Twaddle.

Reality Studios does not store your pronunciation recordings on its servers. Audio temporarily held by the app is discarded after the pronunciation-feedback experience is exited.

Pronunciation assessment results are returned to the app for the purpose of providing feedback. Twaddle does not currently store the pronunciation audio or the resulting pronunciation scores in your Supabase account data.

Microsoft processes audio submitted to Azure Speech Services according to Microsoft’s own terms and privacy practices.

Purchase and Subscription Information

Twaddle offers purchases and/or subscriptions through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which helps Twaddle determine purchase status, entitlements, credits, refunds, subscription status, and related information.

We may process and store information such as:

  • Subscription or entitlement status;
  • Product purchased;
  • Purchase and transaction status;
  • Subscription expiration information;
  • Credit balances and credit transaction history;
  • RevenueCat customer or user identifiers; and
  • Information associated with purchase events, refunds, renewals, and other billing events.

Reality Studios does not receive or store your full credit-card number, CVV, or other payment-card credentials. Payment processing is handled by Apple or Google through their respective app stores.

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may independently process transaction and technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Information We Do Not Currently Collect for Analytics or Advertising

The current version of Twaddle does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not display advertising.

The current version also does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs for tracking user activity.

If these practices materially change in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and any applicable app-store privacy disclosures.

AI and User Conversations

The current version of Twaddle does not provide an AI chat service that sends user conversations or prompts to a generative AI provider.

Some lesson content may have been created with the assistance of artificial-intelligence tools before being included in Twaddle. This content-generation process does not involve sending your personal information, messages, or prompts to an AI model.

Microsoft Azure’s processing of pronunciation audio, described above, is separate from this pre-generated lesson content.

2. How We Use Information

We use information collected through Twaddle to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and maintain user accounts;
  • Provide Twaddle’s language-learning functionality;
  • Personalize lessons and learning experiences;
  • Track lesson progress and vocabulary knowledge;
  • Operate spaced-repetition and review features;
  • Provide pronunciation assessment and feedback;
  • Maintain subscriptions, purchases, credits, and entitlements;
  • Enable offline functionality;
  • Respond to support and privacy requests;
  • Protect Twaddle, our users, and our services from fraud, abuse, and security threats;
  • Diagnose technical problems and maintain the reliability of the Service;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • Enforce our agreements and protect our legal rights.

We do not use your information to send marketing or promotional emails in the current version of Twaddle.

Twaddle may send or facilitate transactional communications that are necessary to operate your account, such as authentication or one-time verification codes.

3. Service Providers and Third Parties

We use third-party service providers to operate Twaddle. These providers may process information on our behalf or as necessary to provide their services.

Supabase

We use Supabase for services including authentication, database infrastructure, server-side functions, and storage of account and learning data.

Information stored through Supabase may include your account identifier, email address, profile information, language-learning progress, vocabulary-learning information, entitlements, credit information, and other application data described in this Privacy Policy.

Microsoft Azure Speech Services

We use Microsoft Azure Speech Services to provide pronunciation assessment.

When you choose to use pronunciation assessment, your recorded audio is transmitted to Microsoft Azure for processing. Reality Studios does not store that recording on its servers.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, and related purchase information.

RevenueCat may process purchase history, transaction information, app-user identifiers, and certain technical information necessary to provide its services.

Apple and Google

Apple and Google may process information when you download Twaddle, use their authentication services, or make purchases through their respective app stores.

Their processing of information is governed by their own privacy policies and agreements with you.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where laws such as the European Union or United Kingdom General Data Protection Regulation require us to identify a legal basis for processing personal information, our legal bases may include:

Performance of a contract. We process information when necessary to provide Twaddle and the services you request, including maintaining your account, tracking learning progress, and providing purchased features.

Consent. We may rely on your consent for certain activities, such as accessing your microphone and processing pronunciation audio, where required by applicable law. You may withdraw permission to access your microphone through your device settings, although pronunciation features will not function without microphone access.

Legitimate interests. We may process limited information where reasonably necessary to operate, secure, improve, and protect Twaddle, provided those interests are not overridden by your applicable privacy rights.

Legal obligations. We may process or retain information where necessary to comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, respond to lawful requests, or establish or defend legal claims.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as your account remains active or as otherwise necessary to provide Twaddle.

You may delete your Twaddle account from within the app.

When your account is deleted, Twaddle deletes the Supabase authentication account and associated user data that is configured to be deleted with the account, including profile information, vocabulary and learning progress, lesson-attempt information, entitlements, credit balances and credit history, and offline-download records.

Certain records may be retained after account deletion where reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

For example, RevenueCat webhook records used as a transaction or audit trail may remain after account deletion after the associated Twaddle user identifier has been removed from those records.

Information may also temporarily remain in infrastructure backups until those backups expire or are overwritten in accordance with our service providers’ backup-retention practices.

Deleting your Twaddle account does not automatically delete records independently maintained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or other third-party providers. Those providers maintain information according to their own legal obligations and privacy policies.

6. Account Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your account from within Twaddle through the app’s Settings.

Account deletion removes your Twaddle account and associated user data as described in the Data Retention section above.

Deleting your Twaddle account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Subscriptions may need to be cancelled separately through the applicable app store.

If you have questions about deletion or believe information associated with you remains after deletion, contact us at support@realitystudios.org.

Reality Studios LLC is based in the United States, and some of our service providers operate or process information in the United States and other countries.

If you use Twaddle from outside the United States, your personal information may therefore be transferred to and processed in countries whose data-protection laws differ from those in your country.

Where required by applicable law, we and our service providers use appropriate mechanisms for international transfers of personal information.

12. Microphone Permissions

Pronunciation practice is optional.

Twaddle requests microphone access only when needed to provide features that require recording speech. You can deny or revoke microphone permission through your device settings.

If microphone access is disabled, you can continue using portions of Twaddle that do not require pronunciation recording, although pronunciation-assessment features will not function.
