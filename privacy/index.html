Privacy Policy for Twaddle

1. Introduction

Twaddle is a language-learning application operated by Reality Studios LLC (“Reality Studios,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a company based in the United States.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you use Twaddle, including the mobile application listed as Twaddle: Language Learning (the “Service”). It also explains the privacy choices and rights that may be available to you.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide information required under applicable privacy laws, which may include, depending on the circumstances, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), applicable United Kingdom data-protection law, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“CCPA/CPRA”), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (“CalOPPA”), Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”), Brazil’s Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (“LGPD”), and other applicable privacy laws.

The applicability of a particular law depends on factors such as your location, our activities, and the law’s statutory requirements.

2. Information We Collect

We seek to limit our collection of personal information to information reasonably necessary to provide, secure, and operate Twaddle.

2.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create or use a Twaddle account, we may process:

  • Your email address;
  • Authentication information associated with your account;
  • An internal account or user identifier; and
  • Information necessary to authenticate you through a supported sign-in provider.

Twaddle currently supports email-based authentication, Google Sign-In, and Sign in with Apple.

If you use Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, the applicable provider may provide us with information necessary to authenticate and associate your account, subject to the information made available by that provider and your settings with them.

Twaddle does not require your phone number or physical address to create an account.

2.2 Profile and Language-Learning Information

We collect and store information necessary to personalize your learning experience and track your progress. Depending on how you use Twaddle, this may include:

  • Your native language;
  • The language you are learning;
  • Your proficiency level;
  • Your learning goals;
  • App preferences;
  • Onboarding status;
  • Microphone or voice-recording consent status and related timestamps;
  • Lesson progress;
  • Words and expressions you have encountered;
  • Vocabulary mastery information;
  • Spaced-repetition information;
  • Review history and scheduling information;
  • Lesson performance, such as known and unknown word counts, lesson ratings, translation reliance, and completion information;
  • Learning context associated with vocabulary, such as a recent lesson sentence or meaning in which a word was encountered; and
  • Information about lessons or sections downloaded for offline use.

We use this information to provide and personalize Twaddle’s language-learning functionality.

2.3 Pronunciation Audio and Microphone Access

Twaddle includes optional pronunciation features that require access to your device’s microphone.

When you choose to use a pronunciation feature, Twaddle temporarily records your speech and transmits the audio to Microsoft Azure Speech Services for pronunciation assessment. Microsoft Azure processes the audio and returns pronunciation feedback to Twaddle.

Reality Studios does not store your pronunciation recordings on its servers. Audio temporarily held by the app is discarded after you leave the pronunciation-feedback experience.

Pronunciation-assessment results are returned to the app so Twaddle can provide pronunciation feedback. Twaddle does not currently persist the pronunciation recording or resulting pronunciation scores in your Supabase account data.

Microsoft processes information submitted to Azure Speech Services according to Microsoft’s own terms and privacy practices.

Twaddle does not use your voice recording to identify you through voice biometrics, for advertising, or to create a voice profile.

2.4 Purchase and Subscription Information

Twaddle may offer subscriptions, credit packs, or other purchases through the Apple App Store and Google Play.

We use RevenueCat to help manage purchases, subscriptions, entitlements, credits, refunds, and related functionality.

We may process or store information such as:

  • Subscription and entitlement status;
  • Products purchased;
  • Purchase and transaction status;
  • Subscription expiration information;
  • Credit balances;
  • Credit transaction history;
  • RevenueCat customer or user identifiers; and
  • Information associated with purchases, renewals, refunds, and similar billing events.

Reality Studios does not receive or store your full credit-card number, CVV, or other payment-card credentials.

Payment processing is handled by Apple or Google through their respective app stores.

2.5 Information We Do Not Currently Collect for Advertising or Analytics

The current version of Twaddle:

  • Does not display third-party advertising;
  • Does not use third-party advertising SDKs;
  • Does not use third-party analytics SDKs to track user activity; and
  • Does not use third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

If these practices materially change, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store privacy disclosures.

2.6 Artificial Intelligence

The current version of Twaddle does not provide an AI chat service that sends user conversations or user-written prompts to a generative AI provider.

Some lesson content was created with the assistance of artificial-intelligence tools before being included in Twaddle. This content-generation process does not involve sending your personal information, conversations, or prompts to an AI model.

Microsoft Azure’s processing of pronunciation audio, described above, is separate from this pre-generated lesson content.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and learning information while your account is active or as otherwise reasonably necessary to provide Twaddle and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

You can initiate deletion of your Twaddle account from within the app through Settings.

When your account is deleted, Twaddle deletes the Supabase authentication account and associated user information configured to be deleted with the account, including:

  • Profile information;
  • Vocabulary and learning progress;
  • Spaced-repetition information;
  • Lesson-attempt information;
  • Entitlements;
  • Credit balances and credit history; and
  • Offline-download records.

Twaddle does not currently maintain server-side AI conversation history.

Certain records may be retained where reasonably necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

For example, certain RevenueCat webhook records used as a transaction or audit trail may remain after account deletion after the associated Twaddle user identifier has been removed from those records.

Information may also temporarily remain in infrastructure backups until those backups expire or are overwritten according to the applicable provider’s backup-retention practices.

Deleting your Twaddle account does not automatically delete information independently maintained by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or other third-party providers under their own legal obligations and privacy practices.

Deleting your Twaddle account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. You may need to cancel an active subscription separately through the applicable app store.

If you have questions about account deletion, contact support@realitystudios.org.
