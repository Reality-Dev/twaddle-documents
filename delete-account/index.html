Twaddle — Account & Data Deletion

Users of Twaddle can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Delete your account from the app

If you still have access to Twaddle:

  1. Open Twaddle.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, select Delete account.
  4. Follow the confirmation prompts.

Your Twaddle account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to Twaddle, you can request deletion of your account by emailing:

support@realitystudios.org

Use the subject “Account Deletion Request” and include the email address associated with your Twaddle account so that we can identify your account and process your request.

We may contact you to verify that you are the owner of the account before completing the deletion.

Data that will be deleted

When your Twaddle account is deleted, we permanently delete the personal data associated with your account, including:

  • Your account and profile information, including your email address and authentication information
  • Your learning profile, including your target and native languages, knowledge level, learning goals, daily goal, and voice recording consent status
  • Your word-knowledge and spaced-repetition progress
  • Your lesson attempt history
  • Your subscription and entitlement status stored by Twaddle
  • Your offline lesson download records

Data that may be retained

Subscription and purchase records: Subscription and purchase records maintained by third-party services, including RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play, may be retained by those services according to their respective data-retention policies. Deleting your Twaddle account does not necessarily delete records independently maintained by those services.

Twaddle does not otherwise retain personal account data after your account has been deleted, except where retention is required by law.

For more information about how Twaddle handles personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.
